Privacy policy

How we handle information when you visit this website.

Last updated: June 2026

Welcome to Nuel's Science Tutorial. Your privacy matters to us, and we're committed to protecting any information you share while using this website. Since our platform doesn't require user accounts or logins, we don't collect personal information such as names, emails, or passwords from visitors.

Information we collect

Our website may automatically collect certain non-personal information to help us improve your browsing experience, including:

  • IP address and general location, for analytics purposes only
  • Browser type and version
  • Pages visited and time spent on the site

This information is used solely to understand how visitors interact with our content and to improve it. We do not sell, share, or distribute any data to third parties.

Cookies

Nuel's Science Tutorial does not use cookies to track individual visitors. However, third-party services such as Vercel, or embedded videos like YouTube, may use their own cookies. We encourage you to review the privacy policies of those services directly.

External links

Our website may contain links to other websites for tutorials, references, or social media. We are not responsible for the privacy practices or content of these external sites, and we encourage visitors to read the privacy policies of any site they access through our links.

Children's privacy

Our website is suitable for all ages. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13. If you believe information about your child has been collected on our site, please contact us and we will remove it promptly.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes to the website or to legal requirements. Updates will be posted on this page along with the date of the latest revision.
